# Cabinet demo: music prompt for Suno

The demo is about 94 seconds of people reading a terminal. The music carries them along without competing for attention: the same family as the promo's track (driving indie rock, confident), held back to a steady underscore, with one lift at the end where the logo lands.

## Settings

- Mode: Custom, Instrumental on.
- Title: Cabinet demo
- Easiest route, if your plan has it: open the promo's track ("One Big Hit") and use Cover or Remix with the style below, so the two films share a sound.

## Style of Music

```
Driving indie rock instrumental, 110 BPM, 4/4, steady and focused, as an underscore. Clean palm-muted electric guitar pattern, tight live drums kept light (closed hi-hat, soft kick), warm bassline, a subtle second guitar layer later. Confident, purposeful, forward motion, never busy, leaves room for reading. Builds gently over the whole piece, then lifts to a full, bright final section and ends on one clean final chord.
```

## Exclude Styles

```
film score, cinematic, dramatic, orchestral, strings, emotional piano, ambient, lo-fi, dance, club, house, techno, EDM, four on the floor, hand claps, corporate, presentation, stock music, elevator music, hold music, lounge, ukulele, whistling, vocals, choir, guitar solo
```

## Lyrics (structure only)

```
[Instrumental]

[Intro: clean guitar pattern alone]

[Verse: light drums and bass join, steady, focused]

[Verse: same groove, a second guitar layer joins quietly]

[Bridge: brief softer moment, guitar and bass]

[Verse: the groove returns, a little fuller]

[Outro: full band lifts, bright and confident, one clean final chord that rings out]

[End]
```
