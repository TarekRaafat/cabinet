None of them told the story. This time the arrangement is the story:

- **One person:** one instrument, alone, playing a riff.
- **A whole team:** instruments join one at a time, each one a teammate taking a seat. The sound fills up layer by layer.
- **Building:** the full band drives forward. Power and energy from live-sounding drums and bass, not from a dance beat or swelling strings.
- **In control:** everything stops dead on one beat (you decide), then the whole band hits together on one big, confident ending.
