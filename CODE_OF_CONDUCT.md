# Code of conduct

Cabinet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in every space the project runs: issues, discussions, pull requests and anywhere else people take part on its behalf.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and leave no room for harassment of any kind.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainer at tarek.m.raafat@gmail.com. Every report is read, kept private, and answered. The maintainer decides what to do, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: from a private word, to a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban.
